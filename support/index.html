support.

most answers are below. for anything else, email info@skriblio.com and a person will answer.

how do i cancel

apple or google handle the subscription, so you cancel it with them. on an iphone, open settings, tap your name, then 'subscriptions' and skriblio. for android, open the play store, tap your profile, then 'payments and subscriptions'. you can also open 'subscription' in the 'you' tab and tap 'manage subscription'. after you cancel, you keep your plan until the end of the time you've paid for. if you cancel at least a day before the free two weeks end, you pay nothing.

how do i get a refund

refunds are handled by apple or google too. on an iphone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the same apple account. for android, open the play store, tap your profile, then 'payments and subscriptions', pick the order and request a refund. we can't see your card or move money, so we can't do it for you.

what it costs, and how the free weeks work

pick a plan in the app and the first two weeks are free, with nothing charged until they end. after that it's $49.99 a year or $9.99 a month, and apple and google show the price in your currency. the plan renews by itself until you cancel it, and if you cancel at least a day before the two weeks are up, you pay nothing.

what happens to my journal if my plan ends

your pages stay exactly where they are, and you can still export or delete everything without a plan. to journal again, pick a plan in the app.

how do i get my journal out

open 'export data' in the 'you' tab. you can download a text file, a pdf laid out to print, or a zip with everything, including your photos and voice notes. it works with or without a plan, even after the free two weeks are over.

how do i delete my account

tap 'delete account' at the bottom of the 'you' tab and confirm. it happens straight away, and your pages, photos, voice notes and banana are all deleted, so export first if you want a copy. if you no longer have the app, the delete account page says how to ask us by email.

new phone

install skriblio and sign in the same way as before, with your email, apple or google. there's no password to remember, and your pages, photos, voice notes, memory tree and banana are all there. the lock, and any pages you blurred or hid, only apply to the old phone, so set them up again on the new one.

where my journal is kept

your pages are saved on your phone first, then in a database in the eu, for as long as you have an account. to see everything skriblio keeps about you, open 'your data' in the 'you' tab.

your memory tree

it's at the top of the 'vault' tab, and it starts as a small sapling that grows as you add favourites. tap the heart next to a page in the vault, and it hangs on the tree as one card with everything on it. the round button in the corner opens it full screen. swipe to turn it, scroll down to go back through the months, and pinch to zoom in. if you take a page out of your favourites, it comes off the tree but stays in your vault.

the typed up words are wrong

open the page, tap 'typed up' under the photo, then 'edit', and fix the word. your phone remembers the change for the next page, and the photo stays exactly as you took it. for a voice note, tap the small pencil next to its words.

typing up a voice note

open the page in your vault, tap 'not typed up' under the voice note, then 'type it up'. an iphone does this by itself, without sending the recording anywhere. on android, voice notes stay as recordings for now, because typing them up isn't available there yet.

face id

turn on 'face id lock' in the 'you' tab. your vault and your pages then ask for face id or your passcode, and home and your banana stay open. on android it's called 'fingerprint lock' and uses your fingerprint or your screen lock. it's off until you turn it on, and it only covers skriblio on that phone. while it's on, the 'from your tree' widget shows nothing from your journal.

journaling with a friend

in the 'you' tab, tap 'invite a friend', type the name your friend knows you by and send the message. it has a link and your friend code, and it works once, for seven days. if a friend sent you their code, tap 'add a friend's code' under friends and type it. you can have up to three friends. a friend sees your banana, your name and if you journaled today, and never a page, a photo or a time. tap a friend to turn their visits off or to remove or report them. a nod from a friend, or two friends who journaled the same day, shows on home only, and nothing buzzes your phone.

journaling something for a friend

tap 'journal', then 'get a prompt' and 'for a friend', or tap a friend in the 'you' tab and then 'journal something for' them. pick who it's for and journal the page, photo, voice note or doodle. when you save it, you choose whether to send it, with a line like 'this made me think of you' if you like. your banana delivers it the next time your friend opens skriblio, and nothing buzzes their phone. you can send each friend one a week, and you can take it back until it's delivered. your friend can take it off their tree, report it from the card, or block you from your name in their friends list.

worlds

turn on 'worlds' in 'appearance' in the 'you' tab, then pick 'the planet', 'the greenhouse' or 'the clouds'. tap things and they move, and pinch with two fingers to look closer. each world has five rare moments that come on some days. stay away a while and your world looks a bit off, and journaling settles it.

your journal in numbers

open 'your data' in the 'you' tab, then 'your journal in numbers'. it shows your pages, your days journaled, your longest streak and a few more things, counted on your phone each time you open it. it sits behind the lock if you have one on.

the desktop companion

it comes with both plans, and it needs the phone app. go to skriblio.com/desktop on your computer, and the download for a mac or a windows computer starts by itself. open it, then open the 'you' tab on your phone, tap 'desktop companion' and 'make a code', and type that code on the computer.

who can use skriblio

skriblio is for anyone aged 16 and over, and a 16 or 17 year old gets the same private journal as everyone else. there are no ads and no profiling, and your pages stay private unless you choose to share one. we don't build it for anyone younger than 16.
