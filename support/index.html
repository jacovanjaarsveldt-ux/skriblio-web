support.

most of it is below. anything else, info@skriblio.com. a person answers, usually the same day.

how do i cancel

subscriptions are handled by apple or google, not by us. iphone: settings, your name, subscriptions, skriblio. android: play store, your profile, payments and subscriptions. the app also has a manage subscription link on the plans screen. cancelling keeps access until the period ends.

how do i get a refund

also apple or google. iphone: reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with the same apple account. android: play store, your profile, payments and subscriptions, the order, request a refund. we cannot see your card or move money, so we cannot do it for you.

how do i get my journal out

you, then export my data. text as one markdown file, a pdf laid out to print, or everything as a zip with your photos as originals and the care log. this works with or without a plan, always, and from the plans screen after the trial.

how do i delete my account

you, then delete my account, then confirm. it is immediate: photos first, then every row that belongs to you. friends in your circle stop being messaged at once. there is no waiting period and no email to write.

what exactly do my friends get

after five quiet days, one message: hi maya. this is sam's banana. sam has not written in 5 days. no pressure. maybe ask how they are. reply mute to stop these. then at most two more, four days apart, and one line when you write again. never a word of what you wrote, never a mood, never a photo.

a friend is getting messages and does not want them

they reply mute, or you remove them in the circle. either one is immediate. their number is purged after 30 days. if something went wrong, write to us with the number and we will confirm it is gone.

a friend replied mute by mistake

mute is final for that invite. send a fresh invite from the circle and they can say yes again.

i want a break from the circle without removing anyone

circle, then pause. thirty days, and the next possible message date is shown so nothing is a surprise. write once and the count resets anyway.

new phone

install skriblio and sign in with the same email. a six digit code arrives, no password. your entries, photos, banana and circle are all there. the old phone signs out when you delete the app.

the transcript is wrong

handwriting is hard. open the entry, the transcript sits under the photo. you can edit the text of the entry, and the photo is always kept as you took it. on device transcription is on the way.

face id

you, then face id lock. on android it is your fingerprint or face, whatever the phone uses. the lock covers the app and the widget shows only the banana and its line, never an entry.

the mac or windows banana

it needs the phone app. in the app: you, pair a computer, make a code, type it on the computer. it is included in both plans. the downloads switch on when the builds are signed: mac, windows.

why 18 and over

a journal and a list of friends to message is personal information under south african law, which treats everyone under 18 as a child needing a parent's consent. we would rather build that properly than badly. for now, adults.

what it costs, and in which currency

two weeks free from your first entry, no card. then $49.99 a year or $9.99 a month, one usd price everywhere. apple and google show it converted at checkout.

privacy questions and requests under popia or gdpr go to the same address. our information officer is the director of steady labs (pty) ltd. we answer within 30 days, usually much sooner. the privacy notice and terms say the rest.
