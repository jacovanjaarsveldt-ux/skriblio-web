support.

three questions cover most of it. anything else, info@skriblio.com. a person answers, usually the same day.

how do i cancel

subscriptions are handled by apple or google, not by us. iphone: settings, your name, subscriptions, skriblio. android: play store, profile, payments and subscriptions. the app also has a manage subscription link on the plans screen. cancelling keeps access until the period ends.

how do i get my journal out

you, then export my data. text as one markdown file, a pdf laid out to print, or everything as a zip with your photos as originals and the care log. this works with or without a plan, always.

how do i delete my account

you, then delete my account, then confirm. it is immediate: photos first, then every row that belongs to you. friends in your circle stop being messaged at once. there is no waiting period and no email to write.

a friend is getting messages and does not want them

they reply stop, or you remove them in the circle. either one is immediate. their number is purged after 30 days. if something went wrong, write to us with the number and we will confirm it is gone.

the transcript is wrong

handwriting is hard. open the entry, the transcript sits under the photo; you can edit the text of the entry itself, and the photo is always kept as you took it. on device transcription with a cloud opt in is on the way.

why 18 and over

a journal and a list of friends to message is personal information under south african law, which treats everyone under 18 as a child needing a parent's consent. we would rather build that properly than badly. for now, adults.

privacy questions and requests under popia or gdpr go to the same address. our information officer is the director of steady labs (pty) ltd. we answer within 30 days, usually much sooner.
