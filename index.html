your life journal, kept for good.

we'll always tell you to write on paper first, in the notebook you already keep. life is part paper and part phone, so skriblio keeps your notebook pages, voice notes, typed pages and photos together.

it's all saved safely online, and your favourites hang on your own memory tree, which grows as you add more.

three ways into one journal.

whichever you pick, it counts as journaling for the day, and it all goes into the same vault.

  • on paperwrite in your notebook the way you always have, then tap 'scan' on a new page and take a photo of it.
  • out loudtalk for up to five minutes after you tap 'voice'. the recording is kept exactly as you said it, and an iphone can type it up without sending it anywhere.
  • on your phonetap 'journal' on the 'home' tab and type. every new page starts with a question for when you don't know what to write, and the dice swaps it for another.
  • photos and doodles'scan' takes any other photo you'd like to keep with the day, and 'doodle' lets you draw on the page with your finger.

your notebook stays on your desk, and its pages come with you.

each page you photograph is saved with that day's entry, and if you'd like, skriblio types up the handwriting so you can search it.

the photo always stays exactly as you took it, and if a word comes out wrong, you can fix it. your phone remembers the change for the next page.

any notebook works, including the one you already have. some journal school formats are made for paper too, so you write by hand and scan the page in.

everything goes into one vault, and it's kept for good.

every page, photo, doodle and voice note has its own row in the 'vault' tab, newest first, under your memory tree.

  • searchtype any word you remember and search finds it, even in handwriting you've had typed up and voice notes you've had transcribed.
  • any daypick a day on the calendar to see what you journaled, or show only your favourites, your photos or your voice notes.
  • on this daynow and then the top of the vault brings back a page from a month, six months or a year ago.
  • kept for goodeach page is saved on your phone first, so you can journal without a signal. then it's kept safely online, and nothing is lost when you change phones.
  • yours to takeexport all of it whenever you like, with or without a plan. nothing is deleted unless you delete it, and a deleted page waits in 'recently deleted' for thirty days.

your memory tree

your favourite memories grow into a tree.

tap the heart next to any page, photo, doodle or voice note, and it hangs on your memory tree at the top of the vault.

it starts as a small sapling and grows the more you add. the newest things hang near the top, the oldest sit down by the roots, and each month has a small tag on the trunk.

  • open it full screen with the round button in the corner. swipe to turn it, scroll down through the months, and pinch with two fingers to zoom in.
  • tap anything on the tree to see it up close, and the pencil takes you to its page.
  • your own banana sits on a branch and keeps you company up there.
  • after sunset where you are, the tree turns to night, with stars, a moon and fireflies in the branches.
  • take something out of your favourites and it comes off the tree, but it stays in your vault.
  • it's saved with the rest of your journal, so the same tree is there when you change phones.

when you don't know what to write, start with a question.

every new page has one at the top, and the dice swaps it for another. 'get a prompt', under the page, has lots more in topics like today, what went right, people and tomorrow. each one comes as a two minute version, a five minute one and a fifteen minute one.

  • what are three things that happened today?
  • what's one thing from this month you want to remember?
  • is there a conversation you keep replaying?
  • how are you different from a year ago?

journal school

it's in the 'you' tab, and it teaches the old ways people have kept journals, from roman emperors to da vinci.

each format shows what its page looks like, where it comes from and how to do it. tap 'start a page like this' and a new page opens with the format laid out for you.

for the formats made for paper, you write by hand and tap 'scan my paper page' to add it to your vault. every format you try gets a stamp, like a passport.

  • formats

    they run from cross writing and the pillow book to the captain's log and the dream log. every one comes with a drawing of its page.

  • reads

    these are short and longer articles on the history of diaries and what research has found about writing things down. one is about samuel pepys and his secret shorthand.

  • make it yours

    if you keep a paper journal, this part has small ideas for it, like washi tape, pressed leaves or the ticket from a concert.

there's no order to follow and nothing to finish. it's there to teach, and it isn't therapy or medical advice.

a pixel banana keeps you company.

the banana you pick lives on the 'home' tab, with a line for the day and the 'journal' button underneath.

it's pleased when you journal and gets restless when you don't. all it knows is which days you journaled, and it never sees what you write.

after your first page each day it shows you an uncommon word or a fact, and there's a small game if you'd like one.

questions

what's skriblio

it's a life journal for your paper notebook and your phone. you can photograph handwritten pages, record voice notes, type pages and add photos, and a pixel banana keeps you company.

do i need a paper journal

no, although we'll always suggest writing by hand first if you can. typing a page or recording a voice note counts just the same.

can i use my paper journal

yes, tap 'scan' on a new page and take a photo of a page from the notebook you already use. if you'd like the handwriting typed up, you can search it later, and the photo stays exactly as you took it.

can i talk instead of typing

yes, tap 'voice' on a new page and record up to five minutes. the recording is kept exactly as you said it. an iphone can also type it up without sending it anywhere, so you can search its words.

what's the memory tree

it's the tree at the top of the 'vault' tab, where your favourites hang. tap the heart next to any page, photo, doodle or voice note to put it there. it starts as a sapling and grows as you add more, and you can open it full screen to look back through the months.

is my journal kept for good

yes, for as long as you have an account. it's all kept safely online, so nothing is lost when you change phones, and nothing is deleted unless you delete it. you can export all of it whenever you like, too.

do i have to journal every day

no, and there's no streak to lose. after two quiet days your banana starts getting ideas, and on the fifth it leaves home. journal anything at all and it comes straight back.

does skriblio send notifications

no, it never does. if you go quiet, your banana turns up on your home screen widget or your computer instead.

does the banana see what i write

no, it doesn't. your banana only knows which days you journaled, and nothing about what's on the page.

what's journal school

it's in the 'you' tab, and it teaches sixteen ways people have kept a journal, from the stoics' evening review to da vinci's mirror writing. there are also reads on the history of diaries and what research has found, and ideas for a paper journal.

is this therapy

no, it isn't. a journal can be useful, but it isn't treatment and it doesn't replace care.

is there a desktop app

yes, the desktop companion puts your banana on your mac or windows screen, and it comes with both plans. it needs the phone app, and pages you write on the computer go into the same vault. the mac version is an early build for now, and windows isn't out yet.

can i get my journal out

yes, with or without a plan. open 'export data' in the 'you' tab and pick a text file, a pdf to print, or a zip with everything in it.

how does the free trial work

when you pick a plan, the first two weeks are free, and nothing is charged until they end. after that the plan renews by itself until you cancel it in apple or google settings. if you cancel before the two weeks are up, you pay nothing.

how do i cancel

apple or google handle the subscription, so you cancel it with them. on an iphone, open settings, tap your name, then 'subscriptions' and skriblio. for android, open the play store, tap your profile, then 'payments and subscriptions'. you keep your plan until the end of the time you've paid for.

who can use skriblio

it's for adults, 18 and over. under south african law a journal counts as personal information, and anyone under 18 needs a parent's consent.
