a journal with a banana in it.

skriblio is a paid journal app for iphone and android. you can type an entry, record a voice note or photograph a page of your paper notebook. a banana companion lives on your home screen.

app storegoogle play

the first two weeks are free, then it's $49.99 a year or $9.99 a month.

three ways to journal.

you can type an entry, say it out loud as a voice note, or take a photo of a page from your paper notebook. skriblio can turn the handwriting into text so you can search for it later, and it keeps the photo exactly as you took it. all three end up in the same journal, and an entry from your phone or your computer sits next to a page from your notebook.

if you already wrote in your notebook today, you can tap 'i wrote on paper' and skriblio marks the day without uploading anything.

a banana that notices.

you pick the banana, and it keeps you company on your home screen and on your phone's widgets. it knows which days you journaled and nothing else, and it never reads what you write. when your journal goes quiet for a few days it gets restless and starts getting up to things, and there's no streak to lose.

a memory tree for your favourites.

tap the heart on an entry and it hangs on your memory tree as a small card with its date. the tree starts as a sapling and grows as you add favourites, and you can open it full screen and scroll back through the months.

something to start from.

there's a new prompt on your home screen every day, and a library of them for when you want a different one. journal school has 16 ways to journal, from morning pages to the captain's log, and each comes with the steps. it also has short reads on the history of diaries and ideas for your paper notebook.

when you're new, 'your first ten' on the home screen lists ten things to try, and each one ticks itself once you've done it.

journal from your computer.

the desktop companion puts your banana on a mac or windows screen. a click opens a new entry, and what you write there goes into the same journal as your phone. it comes with both plans. get the desktop companion.

two weeks free, then a plan.

you pick a plan to start, and nothing is charged for the first two weeks. after that the yearly plan is $49.99 and the monthly plan is $9.99, and both include everything. you cancel in your phone's settings, and the store shows the price in your own currency.

questions.

do i have to journal every day?

you don't have to. a quiet day is fine and there's no streak to lose. your banana just gets restless after a few quiet days.

do i need a paper notebook?

you don't. typing an entry or recording a voice note counts just the same. if you do keep a notebook, a photo of a page goes into your journal with everything else.

what does the banana know about me?

which days you journaled. it never sees what you wrote.

is my journal private?

your journal is encrypted on its way to our servers and while it's stored there. we never sell it or use it for ads, and it's never used to train AI. you can lock it with face id or a fingerprint, export all of it whenever you like, or delete your account from inside the app.

what happens after the two free weeks?

your plan starts by itself unless you cancel before they end. you can cancel any time in your phone's settings.

who's skriblio for?

it's for people aged 16 and over.
