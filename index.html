a journal with a banana in it.

skriblio is a journal app for your phone. you can type a few lines, record a voice note or take a photo of a page in your paper notebook. your banana lives on your home screen, and it notices the days you journal.

app storegoogle play

for iphone and android.

three ways to journal.

some days you want to type, some days you'd rather talk, and some days you've already filled a page in your notebook. skriblio keeps all of it in one place.

  • typetap 'journal' and type a few lines, or start from today's prompt.
  • talkrecord a voice note. it's kept exactly as you said it.
  • scantake a photo of a page in your notebook. skriblio types up the handwriting so you can search for it later.

a banana that notices.

your banana knows which days you journaled. it never sees what's in your journal.

after a couple of quiet days it gets restless. stay away for a while and it goes rogue, and one journal entry calms it straight down. there's no streak to lose.

pick the banana that suits you, and give it a world to live in: a small planet, a greenhouse or the clouds.

  • the planet
  • the greenhouse
  • the clouds

keep your favourite entries on a memory tree.

tap the heart on any journal entry and it hangs on your own memory tree. the tree grows as you keep journaling, and it's yours for life.

journal with friends.

invite a friend to journal with you. now and then their banana pops over to your home to say hello. you can also send a friend something you journaled just for them, and your banana takes it to them.

they never see the rest of your journal.

your journal stays yours.

it isn't sold, used for ads or used to train ai. lock it with face id, export everything whenever you like, or delete your account from inside the app.

do i have to journal every day?

you don't have to. a quiet day is fine, and there's no streak to lose. your banana just gets a little restless.

can i keep journaling on paper?

you can. take a photo of the page and it goes into your journal with everything else.

who can read my journal?

only you can. your banana knows which days you journaled, and nothing else.

what happens after the two free weeks?

your plan starts by itself unless you cancel at least a day before. you can cancel any time in your phone's settings.

who's skriblio for?

skriblio is for anyone aged 16 and over.
