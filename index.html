questions

can i use my paper journal

yes, photograph a page from the notebook you already own and it joins your typed entries in the same vault. skriblio doesn't sell you a notebook, and it doesn't ask you to give yours up.

can skriblio search my handwriting

yes, once you've asked it to type up a page. the text becomes searchable and the original photo stays with the entry, exactly as you took it.

do i have to journal every day

no, and twice a week is plenty. there are no streaks, no badges and nothing counted at you.

what happens if i stop writing

your banana gets restless, and after a while it goes rogue and turns up somewhere else. write again and it comes home. nothing is lost and nothing gets locked.

does the banana see what i write

no, it doesn't. it knows the day you last wrote, and that's the only thing about you it knows.

what's journal school

it's 6 short topics, 14 techniques and 22 research findings, in the app under you. it's educational, and it isn't treatment.

is there a desktop app

a small banana for mac and windows, in both plans. it needs the phone app, and it saves into the same vault.

can i get my journal out

yes, with or without a plan. go to you, then export data, and you get one text file, a pdf to print, or a zip with your original photos.

how does the free trial work

two weeks, and there's no card until you pick a plan. when it ends you can subscribe, or export everything and walk away.

how do i cancel

apple and google handle the subscription. on iphone go to settings, your name, subscriptions, skriblio. on android go to the play store, your profile, payments and subscriptions. you keep access until the period ends.
