how it works

one

write anything

two minutes is enough. or take a photo of the paper page. the handwriting becomes searchable text, the photo stays in your vault.

two

the banana keeps count

it changes when you write and when you do not. a widget, one line, no streaks, no badges, no red dots.

three

the circle

pick up to five friends. after five quiet days the banana sends one whatsapp message. then every four days, three at most. it stops the moment you write.

pick your banana

you did not write. ned left.

the peel stays. so does the door. write once and it comes back.

what the friend gets

  • only that you have not written for a few days. never what you wrote. never a mood.
  • only after they say yes on a page like this one, with the exact words recorded.
  • at most three messages in one silence, four days apart, then one when you are back.
  • they reply stop, it stops. you remove them, it stops. you pause the circle for a month, it waits.

a banana for your desk

a small one, in the corner of your screen. it does nothing most of the day. once or twice, if you have not written, it slides in with one line. click it to write. mac first, windows after. in both plans.
