type it, say it, or scan it.

photograph a page from your paper journal, record a voice note or type a page in the app. it all counts as journaling, and it all goes into the same vault.

  • on papertap 'scan' and take a photo of the page. skriblio can type up your handwriting, so you can search it later.
  • out loudtap 'voice' and talk, and the recording is kept just as you said it.
  • on your phonetap 'journal' and type. every new page starts with a question, in case you're stuck.

your memory tree

the pages you love grow into a tree.

tap the heart on a page and it hangs on your memory tree. it starts as a small sapling and grows as you add more. it stays on the tree for as long as you have an account, so years from now you can climb back through the months.

a question on every new page.

every new page starts with a question, and the dice swaps it for another one. journal school, in the 'you' tab, shows you the old ways people kept journals, from roman emperors to da vinci's notebooks. you can try each one on a page.

leave it long enough and it goes bananas.

your banana lives on your home screen and notices the days you journal.

skip a couple of days and it gets restless, then it runs about, bounces off the walls and stands on its head.

journal anything at all and it calms straight down.

on your home screen, and on your computer.

add the widget and your banana sits on your home screen, so you can see what it's up to without opening the app. on a mac or a windows computer, the desktop companion puts it on your screen while you work.

the iphone widgets sit on a mac's desktop too. that works on macos sonoma or later, when the iphone is near the mac and signed in to the same apple account.

both come with your plan. open skriblio.com/desktop on your computer to get the desktop companion.

your journal stays yours.

your banana only knows which days you journaled, and it never sees what's on the page. nothing in your journal is sold or used for ads, and no model is trained on it.

questions

what's skriblio

it's a life journal for your paper notebook and your phone. you can photograph handwritten pages, record voice notes and type pages, and a banana keeps you company.

do i need a paper journal

you don't need one. typing a page or recording a voice note counts just the same, but if you keep a paper journal, skriblio keeps its pages too.

do i have to journal every day

you don't have to. after a couple of quiet days your banana gets restless, and if you stay away for a while it goes bananas. as soon as you journal anything at all, it calms straight down.

what does the banana know about me

only which days you journaled. it never knows what's on the page.

how long is my journal kept

for as long as you have an account. it's kept online, so your pages are there when you change phones, and you can export all of it whenever you like.

is there a desktop app

yes, the desktop companion puts your banana on a mac or windows screen while you work, and it comes with both plans. open skriblio.com/desktop on your computer to get it.

how does the free trial work

the first time you pick a plan, the first two weeks are free and nothing is charged until they end. after that the plan renews by itself until you cancel it.

how do i cancel

apple or google handle the subscription. on an iphone, open settings, tap your name, then 'subscriptions'. on android, open the play store, tap your profile, then 'payments and subscriptions'.

who can use skriblio

it's for adults, 18 and over.

anything else is on the support page.
