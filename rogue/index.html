rogue mode

leave it long enough and it may go rogue.

skriblio is a life journal for your notebook, your voice and your phone, with a pixel banana living in it. after two quiet days the banana starts getting ideas, and on the fifth it leaves home.

download

pick a plan and the first two weeks are free. after that it's $49.99 a year or $9.99 a month, and both plans include the desktop companion.

what you get

  • skriblio never sends notifications, and there's no streak to keep or make up for.
  • by the fourth day it has packed a bag, and you can make a missing poster to share.
  • while it's away it peeks into the app now and then, and it turns up on your home screen widget or your computer instead.
  • any page brings it straight home, whether it's typed, a photo of your notebook or a voice note.
  • your banana only knows which days you journaled, and it never sees what you write.

the rest of skriblio, from the memory tree to the desk banana, is on the home page.
