rogue mode

leave it long enough and it may go rogue.

it's a private journal with a banana in it. stay away and the banana gets restless, packs a bag, and turns up somewhere you didn't expect. write again and it comes home.

download

the first two weeks are free. after that it's $49.99 a year or $9.99 a month, and apple and google show the price in your currency. the mac and windows banana is included.

what you get

  • there's no streak, no badge and no red dot. twice a week is plenty, and a quiet day costs you nothing.
  • the banana changes with the days since you last wrote, and when it's out it sends a postcard from somewhere new.
  • you can also pick a friend to hear that you've stopped writing, and never a word of what you wrote. they say yes first.

the friend message isn't live yet. the wording and the yes page are ready, and until the number is live nothing goes out on its own.

what a friend gets

  • one plain message on whatsapp after five quiet days, with your names in it.
  • at most two more, four days apart, and then the banana stays out and says nothing.
  • one line when you're back: sam wrote, so i'm home. thank you.
  • there's always a way out. if they reply mute it stops, if you remove them it stops, and pausing waits a month.

what they never get

  • a word of what you wrote, because the banana only knows dates.
  • a mood, a rating, a title, a photo.
  • the app, which friends never need.
  • anything at all before they said yes on a page that shows the exact words, and keeps them.

you can pick up to five people, and each one says yes on their own page first.
