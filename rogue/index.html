rogue mode

leave it long enough and it may go rogue.

skriblio is a private journal with a banana in it. after two quiet days your banana gets restless, and after five it packs a bag and leaves home. journal anything at all and it comes straight back.

download

pick a plan and the first two weeks are free. after that it's $49.99 a year or $9.99 a month, and both plans include the desktop companion.

what you get

  • skriblio never sends a notification, and there's no streak to keep or make up for.
  • while it's away you'll see it on your home screen widget, and you might catch it running across your computer screen.
  • your banana only knows which days you journaled. it never sees what you write.
