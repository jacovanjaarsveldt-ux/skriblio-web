from version 0.1.5 it updates itself, with a small 'update now' when there's a new version. the desktop companion comes with both plans and needs the phone app. both versions are early builds that aren't signed yet, so opening one takes an extra step, which is shown below.

on windows

  1. one

    open it

    open skriblio-setup.exe from your downloads. if your browser says it isn't commonly downloaded, choose 'keep'.

  2. two

    run it

    if windows says 'windows protected your pc', click 'more info', then 'run anyway'. it installs for you only, and it doesn't need an admin.

  3. three

    connect it

    your banana appears at the bottom of your screen, next to a box. on your phone, open the 'you' tab, tap 'desktop companion', then 'make a code', and type the code into that box.

on a mac

  1. one

    open it

    open skriblio.dmg from your downloads and drag skriblio into 'applications'. it runs on a mac with apple silicon or with intel.

  2. two

    connect it

    your banana appears at the bottom of your screen, next to a box. on your phone, open the 'you' tab, tap 'desktop companion', then 'make a code', and type the code into that box.
