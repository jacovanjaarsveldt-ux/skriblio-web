the desktop companion

your banana, on your computer.

pick your computer below, and the download starts.

download for mac download for windows

from version 0.1.5 it updates itself, with a small 'update now' when there's a new version. the desktop companion comes with both plans and needs the phone app.

the mac version is signed and checked by apple. the windows version is an early build that isn't signed yet, so opening it takes an extra step, which is shown below.

on windows.

  1. 1
    open it

    open skriblio-setup.exe from your downloads. if your browser says it isn't commonly downloaded, choose 'keep'.

  2. 2
    run it

    if windows says 'windows protected your pc', click 'more info', then 'run anyway'. it installs for you only, and it doesn't need an admin.

  3. 3
    connect it

    your banana appears at the bottom of your screen, next to a box. on your phone, open the 'you' tab, tap 'desktop companion', then 'make a code', and type the code into that box.

on a mac.

  1. 1
    open it

    open skriblio.dmg from your downloads and drag skriblio into 'applications'. it runs on a mac with apple silicon or with intel.

  2. 2
    connect it

    your banana appears at the bottom of your screen, next to a box. on your phone, open the 'you' tab, tap 'desktop companion', then 'make a code', and type the code into that box.
