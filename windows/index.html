the banana, on windows.

a small companion that sits at the edge of your screen. it is not a second journal. it is the same banana, keeping the same count, one line at a time.

download for windows, after the phone app ships
signed for windows 10 and 11. included in both plans.

how it pairs

one

install

open the download. the banana appears in the system tray and in a corner you choose.

two

show it a code

in the phone app, you, then pair a computer. a six character code appears. type it on the windows.

three

that is all

it now knows if you wrote today. click it and the phone opens to a blank page. a small box to write in on the windows comes later.

what it will not do

no sound. no bouncing. no badge counts. it never moves on its own across the screen. it never shows your journal on a shared display; the words stay on the phone unless you open them.
