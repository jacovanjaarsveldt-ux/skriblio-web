paper

keep the notebook.

take a photo of a page from the notebook you already use. if you'd like, skriblio types up the handwriting so you can search it later, and the photo stays exactly as you took it.

download

pick a plan and the first two weeks are free. after that it's $49.99 a year or $9.99 a month, and both plans include the desktop companion.

what you get

  • your notebook stays your notebook, and skriblio is the index.
  • the first time you scan, skriblio asks whether to type up handwriting, and nothing is sent away for that unless you say yes.
  • you can fix any word that comes out wrong, and your phone remembers the change for the next page.
  • pick a day on the calendar to see its pages, and 'on this day' brings back one from a month or a year ago.
  • some journal school formats are made for paper, so you write the page by hand and scan it in.
