where they are.

  • today's prompt is at the top of the menu when you tap 'journal', and a new one comes each day. you can answer it with 'scan', 'type' or 'voice'.
  • 'get a prompt' opens the library. there's a topic for most kinds of day, like 'on your mind' or 'what's next'. a row of easy prompts takes a minute each.
  • 'how long have you got?' sets the length. pick 2, 5 or 15 minutes, and the prompt asks for that much.
  • 'go deeper' has eight series of six or seven prompts, each on one subject. one is where the money goes, and another is the house you grew up in. you answer them in order, and the last one has a few boxes for what you'll do next. nothing expires, so you can do one now and the next one next week.
  • 'for a friend' is a prompt for something you journal just for someone. when you save it, you choose whether to send it, and your banana delivers it.
