what's new.

every time skriblio changes, it's listed here in plain words, newest first. there's a section for each release of the phone app and of the desktop companion.

november 2026

phone app 1.2.4

  • the first entry is clearer, with three ways to start: type it, say it, or take a photo of a page.
  • 'your first ten' on home lists ten things to try, and a note and a photo are waiting on your memory tree.
  • your banana has new drawn moves on home, in the worlds and on the widgets.
  • on a day you journaled in your notebook, you can tap 'i wrote on paper' to mark it.
  • a new sheet shows how to put your banana on your mac or windows computer.

11 november 2026

desktop companion 1.0.1

  • push your pointer against the right edge of the screen, or click the banana and 'journal', and a slim panel slides out to journal in. it leaves when you move away.
  • your banana jumps onto the panel when you open it to write.
  • one button opens the panel as a window in the middle of the screen for longer writing, and puts it back at the edge.
  • 'not now' sends your banana off until tomorrow morning, in one click.

on windows

  • the panel is new on windows in this version. if it doesn't take your typing on your computer, the page you know opens instead.

11 november 2026

desktop companion 1.0.0

  • 'not now' sends your banana off until tomorrow morning, in one click.
  • the banana is drawn on whole pixels at every size, so it stays sharp on every screen.

on a mac

  • push your pointer against the right edge of the screen, or click the banana and 'journal', and a slim panel slides out to journal in. it leaves when you move away.
  • your banana jumps onto the panel when you open it to write.
  • one button opens the panel as a window in the middle of the screen for longer writing, and puts it back at the edge.
  • saving shows a small tick and the panel is gone.

october 2026

phone app 1.2.3

  • a new first week walks you through your first days, and you pick a world at the start.
  • the plans page is clearer.
  • after you save, your banana makes a move, and the morning after you journal it looks glad to see you.
  • scan corners are easier to drag, and there are more kinds of games.

october 2026

phone app 1.2.2

  • 'go deeper' in the prompts has eight series, each on one subject.
  • there are 150 daily words, and today's word or game also comes after a scan.
  • the memory tree mixes card types and looks calmer.
  • the worlds have more life: a sleeping banana, a lamp, and hops between clouds.
  • the doodle canvas is on android.
  • the plans page is clearer.

october 2026

phone app 1.2.1

  • settings have their own screen.
  • after you journal, home says thanks and shows one tip.
  • if you stay away for a while, the planet slowly dries out and cracks, and it heals when you journal again.

october 2026

phone app 1.2.0

  • your banana can remind you to journal, if you'd like it to, and never more than once a day.
  • there are three new bananas to choose from: the surfer, the hiker and the trucker.
  • help is shorter and easier to read.

21 october 2026

desktop companion 0.1.9

  • the page it opens always has a prompt to start from, with a dice for another one, however you got to the page.
  • when the banana gives you a prompt, 'use this prompt' opens the page with it filled in.

on a mac

  • your screenshots have the banana in them again. it only steps out of the picture while you're on a call, and a dictation app or a music program doesn't count as one.
